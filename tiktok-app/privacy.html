Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Lindor Clip Drafts is used only by its owner with their own TikTok account.

  • Data accessed: the TikTok access token the owner grants (scope video.upload), used only to upload the owner's videos to their TikTok drafts.
  • Storage: tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • No other users: the tool does not collect information about any other TikTok users.
  • Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
